Specialists in public seating with over 60 years of experience

Founded in 1965 by Rodney Kinsman RDI, OMK’s affordable, sustainable, and flexible seating systems have been installed in over 370 projects worldwide. With headquarters in London and a global network of partners we offer a complete range of services including bespoke design, planning, installation and full worldwide after-sales support.

Working with the world’s leading architects, operators and specifiers we incorporate new technology, fresh thinking, decades of experience and feedback from customers and our supply chain into our design process to benefit passengers and operators alike.

Ataturk Airport, Istanbul

9,785 seats

OMK installed Trax with a fully upholstered finish in Istanbul Airport when it opened in 2004 and provided ongoing support, supplying in excess of 6000 seats. As a preferred supplier to TAV, we have gone on to supply seating for many other major international airports including Madinah.

Kigali International Airport, Rwanda

750 seats

OMK were selected by Rwanda Airport Company to deliver and install 750 seats from their award-winning Trax range at Kigali International Airport. The airport selected the low back chassis finished in durable moulded polyurethane which carries a 25 year guarantee.

Eurostar St Pancras Rail Station, London

1,130 seats

OMK were selected to develop a seat for the new Eurostar departure lounge which opened in 2007. Briefed to supply a timeless seat sympathetic to the grand era of rail travel, they placed a focus on luxury materials, comfort and durability. The result was Trax high back in hand-stitched coach hide leather.

Dublin Airport Terminal 2, Ireland

3,877 seats

Working closely with the project architects Pascall + Watson, OMK were engaged to develop a PRM seating solution for use throughout the terminal. Polyurethane was selected for its durable properties and symbols were moulded into the back panels for longevity.

King Abdulaziz Airport, Jeddah

15,000 seats

With an estimated capacity of 80 million passengers per annum, the new terminal at Jeddah is designed to cope with a large volume of passengers. OMK were selected by the engineering team at Arren in conjunction with GACA to develop a durable version of their Trax high back seating system. Re-engineered in moulded polyurethane, GACA ordered 15,000 units which were delivered within a 9 month period.

Guangzhou Jiaoxin Airport, China

15,000 seats

Selected for their large production capacity, OMK manufactured, shipped and installed 15,000 Trax low back seats. These were perforated metal seats with genuine high quality leather. These were shipped and installed within 6 months from their facility in the UK.

Gatwick Airport North Terminal, UK

3500 seats

OMK worked closely with the architect and Gatwick Airport to create bespoke seating zones using a variety of OMK Seating including different configurations of Metro, both backless and high back. Flite was used for higher density seating with PU pads paired with Metros planters to keep the area consistent. Trax and Bridge were also used in outer areas ensuring passengers have access to a wide range of seating types and locations.

Portsmouth Ferry Terminal, UK

76 seats

Portsmouth wanted to create a more natural and brighter terminal gate. OMK created looped runs of Metro that allowed for long planters to fit in the created void. With its oak faced slats and hint of green frame paired with the live plants really showed off Metro’s natural and dynamic aesthetic.
